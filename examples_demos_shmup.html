shmup

A vertical shoot 'em up: Kit's delivery glider over the city at night, against the security's hunters. The city scrolls down on BG1 and wraps every 512 lines; the glider, a pool of hunters and Kit's shots are sprites, and a shot that meets a hunter takes both out.

Screenshot

Controls

Input Action
D-pad move the glider
A fire (a short cooldown between two shots)

SNES Concepts

  • Vertical auto-scroll of a 256×512 scene (SC_32x64) with bgSetScroll
  • Sprite pools: fixed-size arrays of {x, y, active} slots; the glider has OAM slot 0, the hunters 1-8, the shots 9-16
  • Several sprite types in one OAM grid: distinct VRAM regions and distinct sprite palettes (0, 1, 2)
  • AABB overlap test between 32×32 sprites
  • One picture, two directions: the hunter is drawn nose up, like the glider; its OAM attribute's vertical flip turns it to dive
  • Edge-triggered fire with padPressed()

A shot despawns when its y goes below 0. The OAM holds y - 1 in eight bits, so y = 0 is written as 255, a line the PPU never shows: one step further and the shot would come back at the bottom of the screen for a frame. A hit is decided on the shot's centre, (8, 8) in its 16×16 sprite, falling inside the hunter's 20×20 box.

Architecture

VRAM (BYTE / WORD addresses)
$0000 / $0000 BG1 tilemap (SC_32x64, 4 KB)
$4000 / $2000 BG1 tiles (184 unique 8×8)
$C000 / $6000 Sprite tiles (glider + hunter + shot)
CGRAM
0-15 BG1 palette 0 (the city)
128-143 sprite palette 0 (the glider)
144-159 sprite palette 1 (the hunter)
160-175 sprite palette 2 (the shot)
OAM
0 the glider
1..8 hunter pool
9..16 shot pool
