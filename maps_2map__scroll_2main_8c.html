Detailed Description

Scrolling map with animated sprite character.

A large Tiled map scrolls as Kit, the fox-kit courier, walks left and right along a row of rooftops at night. The map engine streams only the visible tiles to VRAM, so the map can be much wider than the screen. The sprite cycles through 8 walk frames and flips horizontally based on direction.

This is the stepping stone between "static background" and "continuous_scroll" — it teaches the map engine basics with a simple player character.

This began as the PVSnesLib "mapscroll" example by alekmaul; the map, its tiles and Kit are ours (devtools/art_assets/build.py writes the Tiled map and its tile sheet from assets/neo-lumen/).

SNES Concepts
  • Map engine: mapLoad / mapUpdate / mapUpdateCamera / mapVblank
  • Camera-follow: sprite position drives the viewport
  • Sprite animation: 8-frame walk cycle with horizontal flip
  • oamSet for simple single-sprite rendering
  • mapGetCameraX() / mapGetCameraY() for screen-relative sprite positioning
What to Observe
  • Press LEFT/RIGHT to move Kit along the roofs
  • The background scrolls to follow the character
  • Kit's walk advances one frame every 4 frames of movement
  • Kit flips horizontally when changing direction

u16 kit_tile ( u16 f)

OAM tile number of frame f of Kit's sheet.

The converter lays the sheet's 32x32 blocks out four to a 128-pixel row of OBJ VRAM: block f starts at tile (f % 4) * 4 + (f / 4) * 64.
