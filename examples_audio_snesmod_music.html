SNESMOD Music and Sound Effects

Tracker music with transport controls, and sound effects fired over it, with SNESMOD, the tracker engine that runs on the SNES's sound processor.

Screenshot

Learning Objectives

After this lesson, you will understand:

  • How SNESMOD works (module-based music on the SPC700)
  • Loading a soundbank, a module, and sound effects
  • Music playback controls (play, stop, pause, volume, fade)
  • Playing an effect over the music, with volume, pan and pitch
  • How the module, its echo and the effects share the SPC700's 64 KB
  • How the build turns .it files into a soundbank (opensnes-music)

SNES Concepts

  • The SPC700 is a separate processor with its own 64 KB of RAM; the CPU only sends it commands through four ports
  • snesmodProcess() every frame sends the queued commands
  • A sound effect is a sample uploaded into the SPC700's RAM after the module, played on a voice of its own
  • The echo buffer takes the top of that RAM: what is left between the module and the echo is all the effects get

The soundbank holds two modules. nocturne is the music. neo_lumen_sfx has no song: it is a list of four instruments, and every sample of a soundbank can be loaded as an effect. soundbank.h names each one after its sample (SFX_JUMP, SFX_LASER…).

Initialization

#include <snes/snesmod.h>
#include "soundbank.h"
snesmodInit(); /* upload the driver to the SPC700 */
snesmodSetSoundbank(SOUNDBANK_BANK);
snesmodLoadModule(MOD_NOCTURNE); /* resets the SPC RAM: load effects AFTER it */
u8 jump = snesmodLoadEffect(SFX_JUMP); /* keep the slot it returns */
snesmodPlay(0);

snesmodProcess() is what sends the commands (play, stop, volume, sound effects) to the SPC700. Without it they wait in a queue of 85 and the newest are dropped once it is full; the music already playing goes on.

Room for the Effects

Effects are copied into the SPC700's RAM right after the module, and the echo buffer sits at the top. opensnes-music inspect says how much is left:

$ opensnes-music inspect music/nocturne.it
music/nocturne.it: "Daveo Nocturne", ... -> 18376 bytes of SPC RAM (30008 free of 48384), 8 BRR sources

The nocturne's echo (a delay of 5, 10 KB of buffer) leaves 30 008 bytes. The four effects take 18 460 of them:

Effect Bytes of BRR
jump 4 150
powerup (cut to 0.8 s) 7 200
laser (cut to 0.5 s) 4 500
confirm 2 610

The driver does not check. An effect loaded past the room is copied over the echo buffer: the echo overwrites the effect and plays its bytes back as noise, with no error anywhere. Count the bytes, or ask inspect, before adding an effect to a game with echo — a longer echo delay takes 2 KB a step.

Build & Run

cd $OPENSNES_HOME
make -C examples/audio/snesmod_music

Then run music.sfc in luna (opensnes run) or any SNES emulator.

Memory Layout

ROM

Bank 0: Code
Bank 1: Soundbank data (its own bank, at its start; more banks if it is larger)
Others: Tiles, tilemaps and other const data (the asset banks)

SPC RAM, as this example leaves it

$0400-$19FF: the SNESMOD driver
$1A00-$61C7: the nocturne: module data and its 8 instruments
$61C8-$A9E3: the four effects (jump, powerup, laser, confirm)
$A9E4-$D6FF: free (11 548 bytes)
$D700-$FEFF: the echo buffer

Exercises

  1. A longer echo: give the nocturne edl 10 in its song message (devtools/audio_assets/songs.py), check inspect again, and see which effects still fit.
  2. Pan: play the effect left or right of the player (pan 0 or 255).
  3. A second song: add a third .it file and switch with SELECT.

Common Issues

No audio output

  • Check snesmodProcess() is called every frame
  • Verify snesmodInit() before snesmodPlay()

An effect sounds wrong, or the echo crackles

  • The effect probably does not fit: see "Room for the Effects"
  • Effects loaded before snesmodLoadModule() are lost: load them after

Build fails converting the module

  • opensnes-music inspect music/song.it says what the converter reads in it
