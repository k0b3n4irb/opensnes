mode7_racing — an F-Zero-style mini game
Screenshot

A hover-bike race on a circuit laid over the city's rooftops, round a lagoon. Kit's bike stays fixed low on screen and the world rotates and scrolls under it — the classic SNES racing camera, driven per frame by the mode7 module (mode7SetAngle + SetCenter + SetScroll). Two rivals leave the line with Kit and ride their lanes; seen from the turning camera, their bikes turn with it.

Input Action
D-pad left/right steer
A accelerate
B brake

The gravel slows the bike to a crawl; the lagoon, its quay and the rooftop props (helipads, air conditioners, tanks, solar panels, dishes) stop it; the two chevron pads, on the right and the bottom straight, throw it forward at 5 px a frame.

ROM mode: LoROM (project default).

SNES Concepts

  • The F-Zero camera: mode7SetCenter(bike) pivots rotation on the bike, SetScroll places it low on screen, SetAngle(heading) spins the world — heading shares the math module's 0-255 angle unit, so it feeds fixSin/fixCos (velocity) and the PPU matrix directly
  • Sprites on a turning Mode 7 plane: the plane shows world = bike + M (screen - pivot), so a rival's world position reaches the screen through the inverse matrix, 2 (cos, sin / -sin, cos) at the scale 0x0080 used here (to_screen() in main.c)
  • A sprite cannot rotate, so each rival's bike is drawn turned five times, 0 to 90 degrees; the H and V flips of OAM give the other twelve of the sixteen directions
  • Fixed-point driving physics: 12.4 positions, 8.8 speeds, surface-dependent speed caps
  • A 16 KB collision class map read from C as a const array: it sits in an asset bank like any const data, and C reads it with its bank
  • Mode 7 VRAM interleave (dmaCopyVramMode7) + OBJ tiles at name base 3; between sprites the lower OAM index is in front, so the bikes are sprites 0-2 and their shadows 3-5

The art

devtools/art_assets/build.py writes res/ from the CC0 art of assets/neo-lumen/ (racing.py):

  • the circuit, 1024x1024 in 227 of Mode 7's 256 tiles, from two PixelLab Wang tilesets placed by the corners of a 16-px vertex grid: asphalt and gravel with a red and white curb, gravel and water with a quay;
  • the rooftop props, 32x32 from PixelLab, on the 16-px grid so that each costs its tiles once; the start line and the boost pads drawn by the script;
  • track_class.bin, the class of each 8x8 tile, and the rivals' lanes (lanes_xy.bin, lanes_heading.bin), 256 points either side of the centre line;
  • the bikes, 32x32 from PixelLab in one palette, the rivals' turned with RotSprite (Scale2x three times, turn, back by the commonest colour).
