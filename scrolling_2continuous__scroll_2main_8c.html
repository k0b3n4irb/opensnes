Detailed Description

A threshold camera over two layers in parallax: Kit runs on the roofs.

Kit runs along a stretch of roofs 512 px wide (BG1, a 64x32 map) in front of the far towers (BG2). The camera follows him without a map engine: while he is between two thresholds of the screen he moves and the world stays; what he goes past one, the world scrolls instead and he is put back on it, so he keeps his place on screen while the roofs go by. BG2 scrolls at half BG1's speed, which is what makes the towers look far away. Scroll registers are written via bgSetScroll(), which defers the actual PPU register write to the NMI handler via a dirty flag.

SNES Concepts
  • Multi-layer parallax scrolling (BG1 + BG2 at independent speeds)
  • A 64x32 background map: two 32x32 pages side by side, 512 px of world
  • Deferred scroll register writes via NMI dirty flags
  • Multiple tile sets and palette slots in VRAM/CGRAM
  • A 32x32 sprite, animated and mirrored by its attribute's H-flip bit
What to Observe
  • Hold RIGHT: Kit runs to x 140 and stays there; the roofs scroll by under him and the towers behind them follow at half the speed
  • At the end of the roofs the camera stops and Kit runs on to the edge
  • Release: Kit stands and breathes; LEFT runs back

Function Documentation

◆ frame_tile()

u16 frame_tile ( u16 f)
static

The OAM tile number of frame f of the sheet.

A 32x32 frame is 4x4 tiles. VRAM rows of sprite tiles are 16 tiles wide, so a frame row of four is one VRAM row of tiles: frame f starts at column (f % 4) * 4 of tile row (f / 4) * 4.

int main ( void )

Entry point – a threshold camera over two layers in parallax.

Loads the roofs (BG1, 64x32), the far towers (BG2, 64x32) and Kit's frames, each in its own VRAM and CGRAM slot. Each frame, the D-pad moves Kit; past a threshold the camera scrolls instead, BG2 at half speed.
