## What we checked against your table

- **Overscan**: no example and no lib path sets SETINI bit 2 (the
  `setOverscan` setter exists in `video.h`, nothing calls it), so no
  `fbhash` moves. Noted for the day one does: a 239-row picture is the
  expected value then.
- **SA-1 register sides**: our C side reads none of CFR / MR / VLBP from
  the S-CPU — `sa1.h` declares them, no lib or example code touches them
  from that side. Our S-CPU init writes SIWP = $FF and the SA-1 boot
  writes CIWP = $FF, both on their own side. No BW-RAM write from either
  side before an enable (we do not use BW-RAM yet).
- **SA-1 DMA / bitmap views / VLBP**: unused by the SDK.

One example moved, and it was our bug, not yours: `color/hicolor_1792`
(krom's HiColor64PerTileRow, ported with a longer H-IRQ handler than his)
rendered in bands on v1.23.0. Your mid-picture CGRAM rule is the
arbiter's (snesdev-wiki: "will write the data to the wrong CGRAM
address"); measuring on v1.23.0, the handler's DMA lands in H-blank only
for HTIME 80..175 and krom's 190 spills past it with our prologue. The
example now runs at 128 and renders byte-identically to its baseline on
both v1.22.0 and v1.23.0. So: v1.23.0 caught a real timing bug the
lenient model hid, no luna issue. The rest of the corpus is a no-op, as
you predicted. The
CIWP/SIWP power-on default `sa1_starfield` depends on stays the one
arbitrated on our side (KNOWN_LIMITATIONS, bit = 1 is write-enable).
