## One thing the gate needs before it can gate our VBlank

`luna profile` reports one row per **label**, and our NMI handler is
several labels: `NmiHandler` (entry), `NmiHandler@vblank_work`,
`NmiHandler@skip_callback`, `NmiHandler@mp5_done`, `NmiHandler@nmi_restore`.
So `--budget NmiHandler=N` judges the entry block only (550 mclk on
`text/print_string`), while the handler's real per-frame cost is the sum
of its child rows (3152 + 782 + 550 + 246 + 132 for that ROM).

The per-frame max of a sum is not the sum of per-frame maxes, so the
harness cannot fold the rows itself from the JSON. Request, in the shape
we would use:

- `--budget NmiHandler=N` (and the `per_frame` of the `NmiHandler` row)
  count every row whose symbol is `NmiHandler` **or** `NmiHandler@<child>`
  as one routine, per frame, before taking max / mean. WLA-DX emits
  `@child` labels for local blocks; a parent symbol is any row whose name
  has no `@`.
- If folding by default would surprise other users, a flag is fine
  (`--fold-children`, or `--budget NmiHandler+=N` for "with children");
  we will take whichever you prefer.
- The child rows can stay in `entries` — they are useful to see which
  block paid — as long as the parent row's `per_frame` is the folded one.

Once folded, our gate is `--budget NmiHandler=<limit>` across the corpus
(`tools/luna-test/`, one orchestrator, limit from a manifest key), the
R4 item of our 2026-09-11 review.
