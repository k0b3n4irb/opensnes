## 3.1 `-from` gates under `--until-frame` — fixed, and wider than reported

Reproduced as you describe. **The cause:** the step that advances the
emulator to a trace's start instruction took its budget from `-n`, and
`-n` keeps its default of 1000 when the run is bounded by `--until-frame`.

It reached further than the CPU and memory traces:

- **CPU and memory traces** starting after instruction ~1000 recorded
  nothing, as you saw.
- **DMA and Super FX traces were affected too,** less visibly: they
  switched on at instruction ~1000 **whatever `-from` said**. Your
  `--dma-trace-from 20000` capture of 17 634 events was really a capture
  from ~1000. On `superfx_3d` to frame 12, v1.30.1 gives the same 16 384
  DMA and 132 762 GSU events for a start at 0, 100 000 or 120 000;
  v1.30.2 gives 16 384 / 66 381 at 100 000 and 0 / 66 381 at 120 000.
  **If a DMA or Super FX capture of yours relied on its start point,
  re-take it.**

**The fix:** under `--until-frame`, the advance stops at the start
instruction or at the end of the target frame, whichever comes first, and
the run still ends exactly on that frame (`print_string` to frame 12:
384 706 instructions with or without traces). A start past the frame
records nothing and does not extend the run. A new integration test
checks that a trace window is the same under `--until-frame` as under
`-n`.

Your recipe now works as documented, and the guide shows it:

```bash
from=$(luna state --until-frame 600 --out - game.sfc | jq '.stats.instructions_executed')
luna state --until-frame 610 --cpu-trace cpu.csv --cpu-trace-from "$from" game.sfc
```

## 3.2 `[asserts.dma]` event cap — removed

Rather than a manifest key for the cap, the cap is gone. `luna test` no
longer stores the DMA trace: it drains it after every frame and keeps
only the counts `[asserts.dma]` needs (unsafe VRAM writes with the first
offender, screen-on VRAM bytes per frame). **A run of any length fits,**
and memory no longer grows with it.

Same verdicts as before wherever the old code could count: identical
messages on Super Mario World, Zelda: A Link to the Past and Super Mario
RPG over 300 frames. Star Fox over 300 frames, which hit the old cap, now
passes, and we checked why against the raw trace: all 870 400 of its VRAM
bytes to frame 300 are written under forced blank.

Your 200-frame Super FX manifests can go back to 200 frames.

## §4 `--trace-writes` markers

Done: `--help` and the guide now say the interrupt markers stay in the
CSV next to the writes: `kind` `N` (NMI, at `$4210`) and `I` (IRQ, at
`$4211`). Count writes on `kind` `W`. On `superfx_3d` to frame 12 with
`210B`: 3 `W`, 10 `N`, 1 `I`, the 14 rows you counted.
